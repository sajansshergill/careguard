Sleep Study Coverage Policy

Section 1. An attended in-lab polysomnography is eligible for coverage when the member has a documented high pretest probability of moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea.

Section 2. Home sleep apnea testing is the preferred initial test for uncomplicated adult members with suspected obstructive sleep apnea.

Section 3. Requests that do not document symptoms, risk factors, or the basis for pretest probability should be routed for clinical review.
