Advanced Imaging Coverage Policy

Section 1. MRI of the lumbar spine is eligible for coverage when the member has documented low back pain persisting for at least six weeks despite conservative therapy, including physical therapy or a trial of NSAIDs.

Section 2. MRI is not eligible for coverage as an initial evaluation for uncomplicated acute low back pain of less than six weeks duration in the absence of red-flag symptoms.

Section 3. Red-flag symptoms that support immediate advanced imaging include suspected malignancy, infection, cauda equina syndrome, or progressive neurologic deficit.

Section 4. Requests missing documentation of the duration of symptoms or of prior conservative treatment should be routed for clinical review rather than denied outright.
