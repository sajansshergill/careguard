Orthopedic Procedures Coverage Policy

Section 1. Knee arthroscopy is eligible for coverage when the member has documented mechanical symptoms and has completed at least six weeks of conservative management, including physical therapy.

Section 2. Coverage is not supported for knee arthroscopy performed solely for osteoarthritis without mechanical symptoms, as evidence does not support benefit in that population.

Section 3. Documentation must include the results of prior conservative therapy and relevant imaging findings.

Section 4. Where conservative therapy has been attempted but its duration is unclear, the request should be routed for clinical review.
